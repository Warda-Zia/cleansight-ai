# CleanSight AI
## Smart Waste Monitoring & Response Platform

### Detect. Locate. Respond. Building a Cleaner, Smarter Lahore.

CleanSight AI is an AI-powered Smart Waste Monitoring and Response Platform designed to detect illegal dumping and open burning, locate environmental incidents, and support faster environmental response.

## Problem

Illegal dumping and open burning are persistent urban environmental challenges. These incidents can occur across large urban areas and may be difficult for authorities to identify, verify, locate, and respond to efficiently.

CleanSight AI addresses this challenge by combining Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, GIS mapping, monitoring technologies, and citizen reporting into one integrated platform.

## Solution

The platform follows an end-to-end workflow:

Data Capture
↓
AI Detection
↓
Location Mapping
↓
Intelligent Alert
↓
Authority Response
↓
Environmental Analytics

## Project Workflow

1. Environmental data is captured through cameras, drones, IoT devices or citizen reports.
2. AI analyzes visual information to identify potential illegal dumping or open burning.
3. Detected incidents are mapped using GIS.
4. Relevant alerts are generated.
5. Authorities can review and prioritize incidents.
6. Incident information can be used for environmental analytics and future planning.

## Why CleanSight AI?

CleanSight AI does not stop at detecting environmental violations.

It connects:

**Detect → Locate → Alert → Respond → Analyze**

The goal is to transform environmental monitoring from a reactive process into a more proactive, data-driven approach.
